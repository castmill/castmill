# Notifications

The dashboard tells you about invitations and important events in your organization through the notification bell in the top bar. Notifications arrive live while the dashboard is open; you don't need to reload the page.

## Reading notifications

The bell shows the number of unread notifications. Click it to open **Notifications**.

- Click a notification to open the page it refers to, for example the device that was registered. The notification is marked as read.
- Click **Mark all as read** to clear the unread count.

When you have none, the list shows "No notifications yet".

## Notification types

| Notification | Sent when | Who receives it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Organization Invitation** | Someone invites you to an organization | You |
| **Team Invitation** | Someone invites you to a team | You |
| **Invitation Accepted** | A person joins the organization or a team | The organization or the team |
| **New Device Registered** | A device is registered | The organization |
| **Device Removed** | A device is removed | The organization |
| **New Image Uploaded**, **New Video Uploaded** | A media file you uploaded has been processed and is ready | You |
| **Member Removed** | A member is removed from the organization | The organization |
| **Removed from Team**, **Team Member Removed** | A member is removed from a team | The removed member, and the team |

Organization notifications can be limited to certain roles.

:::note
The dashboard does not send notifications when a device goes offline or comes back online. Check the **Online** column on the Devices page and the device's **Events**. See [Devices](./devices.md#events).
:::

## Invitations

An invitation notification links to the invitation page, where you accept it. You can also accept organization and team invitations from the link in the invitation email. See [Teams](./teams.md) and [Users and teams](../concepts/users-and-teams.md).
